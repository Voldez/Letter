For You

A note from the heart

Haii, Sayangg! This letter might be simple, and the words within it nothing particularly extraordinary, but thank you for being a part of my story. I always pray that your days are filled with good things. If, at some point, the world feels exhausting, remember how far you’ve come that is something to be proud of. Don't forget to smile, take good care of yourself, and keep believing that beautiful things are waiting for you ahead. I want to apologize to you if, during the time you’ve been with me, you haven’t found what you needed from me. I also apologize if I’ve been acting out or overreacting; I never intended to make you angry or annoyed. And the reason I keep asking if we could get on a call even if we just sit in silence is actually my way of feeling your presence after a truly exhausting day. But I know that I’m not the only one who’s tired; you’re even more exhausted than I am, and you’re dealing with your own set of challenges, so I would never force you. But most importantly, I just want us to stay connected—I don’t want us to be apart for too long. To be honest, sometimes I feel a bit envious of the people around you; they get to communicate with you constantly, while I have to wait not that I can’t wait; of course I can it’s just that there’s something that keeps going through my mind. I hope you understand. I hope that after reading this letter, you don’t end up thinking that I’m being excessive or that I’m unable to understand your situation. And if you feel that I’m truly not enough for you, please just let me know.

With warm wishes, Alyan ♡
